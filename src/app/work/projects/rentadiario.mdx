## The Challenge

Rentadiario aimed to become the "Colombian Airbnb," requiring a platform capable of handling thousands of properties, complex pricing rules (seasonality, weekend rates), and instant bookings. The existing solution needed a complete overhaul to improve SEO, load times, and user trust during the checkout process.

## Architecture & Performance

My primary focus was migrating and structuring the application to be scalable and performant.

- **Next.js Implementation:** Leveraged Server-Side Rendering (SSR) for property pages to drastically improve SEO ranking on Google, crucial for organic traffic.
- **Component System:** Built a modular UI library ensuring consistency across the "Guest View" (search & booking) and the "Host Dashboard" (management).
- **Performance:** Optimized Core Web Vitals by implementing lazy loading for high-resolution property images and code-splitting heavy bundles.

## Key Feature: The "Host Calendar"

The most complex engineering challenge was the availability management system.

- **Complex State Management:** Developed a custom calendar engine (removing reliance on heavy 3rd party libraries) to handle date blocking, price overrides, and reservations.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Implemented logic to handle race conditions where two users might try to book the same dates simultaneously.
- **Synchronization:** Created a robust syncing mechanism that updates availability in real-time, preventing double bookings.

## Fintech & Integrations

Trust is the currency of PropTech. I built a secure and flexible payment infrastructure.

- **Multi-Gateway Checkout:** Integrated **Wompi** and **PSE** for local Colombian payments, alongside **Stripe** for international travelers.
- **Automated Payouts Logic:** Developed the frontend logic to display payout statuses and transaction history for hosts.
- **Headless CMS (Strapi):** Decoupled marketing content from the codebase. This allowed the marketing team to launch landing pages and update SEO tags without requiring engineering deployments.

## Impact

- **Scalability:** The platform successfully handled a 300% increase in property listings without performance degradation.
- **Conversion:** Reduced checkout drop-off rates by simplifying the booking flow and adding clear feedback states during payments.
- **Efficiency:** The CMS integration reduced "content-update" tickets for developers by nearly 100%.
