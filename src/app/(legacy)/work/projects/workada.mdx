## Overview

Workada connects professionals with AI training work: experts get paid to complete tasks that improve AI models. With 1,500+ active contributors and 9M+ tasks a year, the site has to earn the trust of both the professionals joining and the companies behind the work.

## What I worked on

As a fullstack developer I worked with **Next.js**, **Sanity**, **Tailwind** and **GSAP**.

- **Headless CMS with Sanity:** content modeled so the team can create and update pages without needing a deploy.
- **Next.js frontend:** pages rendered from Sanity content, optimized for speed and SEO.
- **UI with Tailwind:** a consistent component system for the brand across the whole site.
- **Motion with GSAP:** interactions with personality, like the rolling-text button hover that inspired the one on this portfolio.
