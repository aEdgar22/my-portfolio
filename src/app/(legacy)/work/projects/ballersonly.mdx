## Overview

Developed the frontend architecture for a luxury marketplace. The main challenge was to move away from rigid templates to a custom logic-heavy interface that could handle dynamic pricing based on seasonality and guest count.

## Key Achievements

- **Dynamic Pricing Engine:** Built a custom calculator in React that adjusts quotes in real-time.
- **Payment Migration:** Successfully migrated the checkout flow from Stripe to Authorize.net to meet high-risk processing requirements.
- **UX Optimization:** Reduced friction in the booking process, resulting in higher conversion rates for high-ticket rentals.
