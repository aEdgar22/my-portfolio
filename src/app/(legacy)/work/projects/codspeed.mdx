## Overview

CodSpeed catches performance regressions before they get merged: it runs benchmarks in CI and reports the impact directly on every pull request. Its users are engineering teams who care about milliseconds, so the product's own web had to feel just as fast and precise.

## What I worked on

As a fullstack developer I built and maintained parts of the web experience with **Next.js**, **Tailwind** and **GSAP**, deployed on **Vercel**.

- **Next.js pages:** built with a focus on speed and SEO, which matters when your audience literally measures performance.
- **UI with Tailwind:** reusable components that kept the CodSpeed brand consistent across sections.
- **Motion with GSAP:** animations that explain the product (benchmarks, regressions, PR reports) without hurting load times.
- **Vercel deploys:** a preview for every PR, so changes were reviewed live before shipping.
